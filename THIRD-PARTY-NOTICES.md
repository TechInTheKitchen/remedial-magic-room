Unmodified browser distributions are vendored in assets/vendor/. Original full license texts accompany deployments. No image assets are downloaded: pip textures are generated on the device. Package archives fetched from registry.npmjs.org/three/-/three-0.180.0.tgz and registry.npmjs.org/cannon-es/-/cannon-es-0.20.0.tgz. Versions are pinned; upgrades require replacing vendor files and retaining license notices.

RoundedBoxGeometry.js is also from Three.js r180 under the same MIT license. Its module import was changed to a local relative path; the rest of the helper is unmodified.
